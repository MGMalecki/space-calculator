# 🚀 Space Calculator

A free times-tables game for kids. Fly a rocket from planet to planet by answering multiplication questions up to 10 × 10.

## ▶️ Play now

**https://mgmalecki.github.io/space-calculator/**

It works in any web browser, on a phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to install and no account needed.

## How to play

1. Type your nickname and press **Start**.
2. Answer each question with the number pad. You have 15 seconds per question.
3. Get **5 right in a row** ⭐ to fly to the next planet: 🌕 Moon → 🔴 Mars → 🪐 Saturn.
4. Finish Saturn to fly home to Earth 🌍 and win!
5. Watch out: **3 wrong in a row** ❌ means game over.

Try to beat the 🏆 **Records**: the 8 fastest journeys are saved on your device.

## Add it to your phone like an app

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap ⋮, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
